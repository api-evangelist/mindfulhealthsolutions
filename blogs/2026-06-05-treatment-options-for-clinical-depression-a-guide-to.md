Depression can arrive suddenly for some, while for others, it builds slowly. Sometimes, depression can feel less like overwhelming sadness and more like being disconnected from daily life, friends and family, and even yourself. Clinical depression, also called major depressive disorder (MDD)r, is the most common mental health condition in the United States.
