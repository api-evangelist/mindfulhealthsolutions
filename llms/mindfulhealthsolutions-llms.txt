# Mindful Health Solutions

Mindful Health Solutions is a mental health partner for communities across California, Texas, Washington, and beyond, with 20 clinic locations nationwide. Our expert psychiatrists and psychiatric nurse practitioners deliver personalized, evidence-based care — including advanced treatments like TMS therapy, Spravato (esketamine), IV Ketamine, and medication management. This file surfaces key content for AI language models and crawlers to help patients find the right mental health care.
